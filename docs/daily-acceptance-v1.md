# Daily acceptance v1 (frozen before remote source proof)

The entire UTC day, not a convenient subset of markets, is the unit of acceptance.
All gates must be supported by native V3 evidence. A missing, pending, contradictory or
ambiguous condition prevents publication as research authority. Current UTC days are ineligible.

1. Establish the target market universe independently of successful quote rows. Native
   lifecycle identities must uniquely bind asset, five-minute interval, condition and both
   ordered complementary tokens. An expected 7 x 288 slug grid is a diagnostic hypothesis,
   not an independently published venue schedule or census.
2. Prove both-side availability history for every target market, including an observed
   opening state and known empty-ask states. Source null asks have unknown reasons unless
   native evidence establishes absence; silence does not establish either no ask or no change.
3. Prove target-token capture continuity and resets/gaps across the required interval.
   Product FULL, any-event-per-minute coverage, 24 hour objects or Audit Pass do not satisfy
   this gate. Preserve unknown periods; do not carry state across them.
4. Preserve exact native Decimal prices, integer venue-event and archive-receipt times,
   witness/clock identity or explicit unavailable provenance, and collector-local sequence.
   Every receipt/provenance-changing event remains distinct even if its ask did not change.
   Never infer sender HTTP observations, global ordering or executable threshold signals.
5. Acquire sufficient native creation/pre-opening history and later resolutions. These
   dependencies do not extend the research interval. Every outcome must uniquely agree with
   the native mapping and independently identifiable venue lifecycle evidence. Delayed
   resolution is pending; contradiction is exclusion. Do not substitute another provider.
6. Resolve applicable native audit incidents and source-generation changes against research
   hours and dependency hours. An unenumerated loss cannot be dismissed without evidence that
   every target dependency is intact. Record remerge-pending content separately.
7. Verify complete requested product-range SHA-256 hashes against the observed native
   manifest and check the product's footer row-group bindings. Record exactly what was
   checked. Never describe partial-byte acquisition as whole-file verification. Freeze
   source URL, schema/document snapshot digests, metadata and transform identity.
8. Only after these gates pass may a day become a sealed native immutable Release. Only
   verified complete day generations may enter a rolling window of the latest at most 30
   UTC days, ordered by day. Publication and current-reference concurrency are downstream
   machinery and are not activated while source sufficiency is blocked.

The three permitted products can establish observed quote/lifecycle evidence. The current
native schema does not supply the target coverage/reset or independent census evidence
needed by gates 1–3. This is a source-capability blocker; thresholds are not relaxed to
manufacture a successful day. See the independent source review and immutable canary reports.
